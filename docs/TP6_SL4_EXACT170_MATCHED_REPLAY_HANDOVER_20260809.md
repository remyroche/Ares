# Exact 170-symbol TP6/SL4 matched residual replay

## Outcome

The matched replay was completed on the approved 170-symbol universe using
the exact TP6/SL4/H12 label contract and the same rows for every arm. It does
not support positive economic ranking in this universe/period: all global
top-1/5/10 tails remain net-negative. The additions improve the result
relative to the base-only model, but do not clear the 100 bps cost floor.

The best matched arm is `support_ood+uncertainty` at top-5%:

| Arm | Top 1% net | Top 5% net | Top 10% net | Rank IC |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Base | −158.75 | −121.32 | −113.74 | −0.0059 |
| Base + single-residual family control (not canonical consensus) | −116.37 | −116.89 | −109.93 | −0.0060 |
| Base + support/OOD | −119.33 | −114.12 | −110.84 | −0.0050 |
| Base + uncertainty | −113.31 | −111.21 | −112.85 | −0.0067 |
| Base + support/OOD + uncertainty | **−106.71** | **−105.87** | **−109.59** | −0.0058 |

These are exact-contract, full-170, pooled-global tails. Net is gross minus
100 bps, applied exactly once by the label materialiser.

## What was verified about the residual models

The residual models are fresh monthly fits, not reused fitted model files. They
reuse the existing canonical residual *model family and training contract*:

- native LightGBM LambdaRank;
- 4-hour UTC × side query groups;
- prior-OOF, per-row net residual around a train-only isotonic base-score → net
  map;
- ordinal residual grades `[-150, -50, +50, +150]` bps;
- 75% base rank / 25% residual rank blend;
- depth 4, 12 leaves, learning rate 0.04, 120 trees, feature/bagging fraction
  0.8, L1 1, L2 10, max bin 63, gains `[0, .25, 1, 3, 7]`;
- the canonical dynamic child floor `max(120, ceil(3% × prior-OOF rows))`.

The new outputs are generated by a new monthly side-local base model using the
same canonical base parameter family (140 trees, learning rate 0.05, 31
leaves, minimum child 350, 0.8 subsample/feature fraction, L2 8), trained on
the exact R3-style target. The additions are only:

- causal support/OOD summaries from the raw meta-context spine;
- base-head uncertainty (`p_clear`, `p_adverse`, `p_weak`, entropy, top-2
  margin, conviction).

Thus the run verifies “existing residual architecture + additions”, but it is
not a bit-identical replay of the older fitted canonical artifact: the older
artifact contains precomputed consensus-head fields that are not present in
the exact 170 raw candidate panel. The matched control is therefore the
canonical residual family with no new support/OOD or uncertainty block.

## Exact data and label contract

- Universe: approved 170-symbol CSV.
- Months: January–July 2026; January is base warm-up, so scored OOS months are
  February–July.
- Candidate rows: 941,156 unique rows; 4,705,780 arm prediction rows.
- Complete labelled rows: 533,418 unique rows; 2,667,090 arm rows.
- Decision time: Pack-B signal close.
- Entry: exact next-one-minute open at signal close + 1 hour.
- Geometry: TP `+6 ATR`, SL `−4 ATR`, horizon 12 hours.
- ATR: causal Wilder ATR(14) from completed hourly candles.
- Cost: 100 bps once; `net = gross − 100 bps`.
- R3 base target: robust clear means pre-adverse MFE clears cost + 25 bps
  before the adverse barrier; invalid/incomplete rows are excluded from
  target fitting and tails, not encoded as economic failures.

## Feature contract

The raw hourly store was joined at the exact candidate timestamp, with no
forward/as-of future fill. The configured families contain 311 requested
fields. The store supplied 137 fields for this replay:

- base: 52 available configured direct-base fields;
- meta/context: 85 available configured regime/context fields.

Every symbol had a feature-store file. The remaining configured names were not
silently substituted; they are listed in `run_manifest.json` as unavailable in
the store schema.

At row level, 94.42% of scored rows have at least 90% of the requested
store-backed fields present (93.6% in February, 94.3% in March, 94.1% in
April, 94.0% in May, 96.1% in June, and 95.0% in July). Missing cells are
handled by the established numeric-matrix missing-value path; they are not
filled from future rows.

## Interpretation

1. The residual idea is not falsified by this run alone: the control and all
   additions are negative under a much harsher, exact contract and a universe
   whose early months have severe path/ATR coverage loss.
2. The model family is not the main differentiator here. Adding support/OOD and
   uncertainty narrows the loss, with the union best at all three principal
   tails, but its rank IC remains approximately zero/negative.
3. The exact 170 replay is not comparable to the earlier positive canonical
   2025 result without controlling for target, universe, period, and raw
   feature substrate. The earlier canonical support/OOD result was +39.93 bps
   top-5 net on its 2025 long-only development contract; this exact replay is a
   different transport test.
4. Before changing the residual objective, fix or explicitly gate the early
   missing-ATR/incomplete-path population and rerun the exact contract on a
   stable complete-path window. Then test bounded residual shrinkage rather
   than allowing a free-standing residual ranker to reorder the full universe.
