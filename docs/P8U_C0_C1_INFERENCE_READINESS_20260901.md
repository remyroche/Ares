## Ledger maintenance protocol

This file is the authoritative human-readable status ledger until the
successor is either approved for inference or explicitly retired. Update it
only after a material, receipt-backed event:

1. a frozen artifact, state, source, feature, model, or policy contract is
   created, advanced, superseded, or fails validation;
2. a matched OOS/replay stage completes, including its comparison to the
   retained control;
3. an inference-parity, source-coverage, or one-minute exit audit completes;
4. after a separately authorized inference activation, each hourly decision
   receipt is finalized, including its proposals, orders, position count, and
   entry/exit monitor heartbeats.

The ledger must not infer operational health from a missing receipt. Until an
inference bundle is sealed, the operational table remains a deliberate
"not activated" record rather than a zero-trade claim. Every numeric metric
added below must name its replay contract, population, and portfolio/admission
rules; raw top-k diagnostics are never substituted for executable results.

The associated thread-bound maintenance review is
`p8u-c0-c1-inference-readiness-ledger`. It is an hourly, failed-runs-only
heartbeat that may update this document from immutable evidence, but has no
inference, exchange, order, or contract-changing authority.

## Canonical candidate and selection semantics

```text
complete point-in-time universe
-> Router top 50% identities
-> F72 Base / Under-F120 scores
-> independent C0 and C1-LVA expected-policy-net maps
-> both admitted, then C0-only, then C1-LVA-only
-> within tier: selected family mapped EV
-> ordinary portfolio auction and rich parent policy
```

The tier order is a capacity-ordering rule. It does not permit a model to
manufacture an admission: C0-only and C1-only rows must individually meet the
frozen threshold for their corresponding map. The selector implementation and
the research receipt are documented in
[`C0_C1_AGREEMENT_TIER_CANONICAL_INFERENCE_20260901.md`](C0_C1_AGREEMENT_TIER_CANONICAL_INFERENCE_20260901.md).
