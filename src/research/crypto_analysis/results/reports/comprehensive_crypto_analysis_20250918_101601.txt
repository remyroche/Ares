METHODOLOGY: COMPOSITE TRADING OPPORTUNITY SCORE
==================================================
The Composite Score combines three weighted factors to rank trading opportunities:

1. PROFIT SCORE (40% weight):
   - Measures average profit per successful trade
   - Formula: avg_profit / 0.02 (normalized to max expected ~2%)
   - Higher profits = better score

2. FREQUENCY SCORE (40% weight):
   - Measures how often trading opportunities occur
   - Formula: success_rate / 0.30 (normalized to max expected ~30%)
   - More frequent opportunities = better score

3. CONSISTENCY SCORE (20% weight):
   - Measures reliability of opportunities
   - Formula: min(1.0, total_trades / 20,000)
   - More total trades = more consistent pattern

FINAL FORMULA: (Profit_Score × 0.4) + (Frequency_Score × 0.4) + (Consistency_Score × 0.2)

DAILY PROFIT POTENTIAL CALCULATION:
  Formula: Average Profit per Trade (%) × Number of Daily Opportunities
  Daily Opportunities = Total Opportunities ÷ Number of Days in Dataset
  Example: 18,544 total opportunities ÷ 730 days = 25.4 opportunities/day
  Daily ROI = 1.44% profit × 25.4 opportunities = 36.6% potential daily ROI
  Note: This represents the maximum theoretical daily ROI if all opportunities are captured

EXAMPLE - ALGOUSDT:
  Profit Score: 1.44% / 2% = 0.720
  Frequency Score: 26.5% / 30% = 0.883
  Consistency Score: min(1.0, 18,544/20,000) = 0.927
  Composite: (0.720×0.4) + (0.883×0.4) + (0.927×0.2) = 0.827
  Daily Opportunities: 18,544 total ÷ 730 days = 25.4 opportunities/day
  Daily ROI: 1.44% × 25.4 opportunities = 36.6% potential daily ROI

INTERPRETATION:
  Score 0.8+: Excellent trading opportunities
  Score 0.6-0.8: Good trading opportunities
  Score 0.4-0.6: Moderate trading opportunities
  Score <0.4: Limited trading opportunities
