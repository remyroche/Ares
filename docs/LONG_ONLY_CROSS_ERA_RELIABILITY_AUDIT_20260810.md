# Long-only cross-era reliability audit

Scope: longs only. Short rows are excluded from all metrics and conclusions.

## Purpose

The July–November audit showed positive pooled net tails and a very strong November holdout for several reliability corrections, while July–October remained negative. This audit applies exactly the same fixed boundary arms to the older primary strict-OOS months (September 2023–February 2024) to determine whether the apparent improvement is portable across eras.

No new target, feature, HPO, or side mapping was introduced. The existing frozen ATR2/q4h score and causal expanding-month reliability learner were reused.

| Nov–Dec 2023 | −69.13 | ±100 α=.50 | −7.02 |
| Jan–Feb 2024 | −10.37 | ±50 α=1 | **+92.81** |
| Jul–Oct 2024 | −63.79 | Raw control | −63.79 |
| Nov 2024 | −13.71 | ±50 α=1 | **+119.33** |

The positive pooled result is therefore not a uniform cross-era effect. It is mainly generated by January–February 2024 and November 2024. The earliest era and July–October remain materially negative.

## Conclusion

There is positive long-only net EV somewhere in this pipeline, including a positive all-era pooled top-5 for ±50/α=1. However, the cross-era evidence does **not** justify promoting a fixed reliability boundary:

1. The pooled gain is concentrated in January–February 2024 and November 2024.
2. The earliest primary era remains negative.
3. July–October 2024 remains negative, and the predeclared development selection still chooses the raw control.
4. Top-10 net EV is negative across all eras and all tested arms.

The next repair should therefore be a causal regime-conditioned conversion/admission map with shrinkage, selected only on prior eras and validated on a new untouched era. A single global ±50/±75/±100 residual boundary is insufficient.
