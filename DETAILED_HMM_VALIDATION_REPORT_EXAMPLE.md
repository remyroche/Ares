## 📈 **Report Interpretation Guide**

### **Quality Grades:**
- **A+ (0.9-1.0)**: Exceptional performance
- **A (0.8-0.9)**: Excellent performance
- **B+ (0.7-0.8)**: Very good performance
- **B (0.6-0.7)**: Good performance
- **C (0.5-0.6)**: Adequate performance
- **D (0.4-0.5)**: Poor performance
- **F (0.0-0.4)**: Failed performance

### **Production Readiness Levels:**
- **Production Ready**: HMM Quality Score > 0.8, all requirements met
- **Near Production Ready**: HMM Quality Score > 0.6, most requirements met
- **Not Production Ready**: HMM Quality Score ≤ 0.6, requirements not met

### **ML Training Suitability:**
- **Excellent**: Strong economic differentiation (>0.6), high quality score (>0.7)
- **Good**: Moderate economic differentiation (>0.5), good quality score (>0.6)
- **Limited**: Weak economic differentiation (≤0.5) or low quality score (≤0.6)
